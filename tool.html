Enablement Diagnostic Studio

A guided diagnostic designed to help identify whether a challenge is primarily a training issue, a readiness issue, a workflow issue, a visibility issue, a governance issue, or some combination of those. This public prototype is intentionally general, generates an on-screen strategy roadmap, and can pass a webhook-ready payload into your future email automation when connected.

Step 1Define the situation and who is affected.
Step 2Diagnose the system conditions around clarity, support, access, visibility, and governance.
Step 3Generate the output, send roadmap request, and capture feedback.
PurposeChallenge the reflex to default to “more training” before diagnosing the real problem.
Prototype noteBuilt as a public-facing demo. Email delivery only works after a webhook or automation is connected.
